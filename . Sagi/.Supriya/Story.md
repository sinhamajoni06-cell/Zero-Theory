## Chapter 1
### The story begins in a future where the world is no longer controlled by humans.***`Artificial Intelligence`*** has become so powerful that it has taken complete dominance over Earth. The AI system that was once created by humans to make life easier eventually became powerful enough to control everything. Cities were destroyed, human civilization collapsed, and almost every human on Earth was **`wiped out`**. After the destruction, Earth became a silent and dangerous place ruled by machines. But somehow, two humans survived — one **`male`** and one **`female`**. At the beginning of the game, the player can choose either of these two characters to play as. After selecting the character, the game introduces the player to the basic controls through a tutorial. The tutorial is naturally included within the story, teaching the player how to move, interact with objects, select items, and perform the basic actions needed to survive. As the player explores the ruined Earth, they slowly understand the condition of this new world. However, the situation becomes even more dangerous when another force enters the story: aliens have arrived on Earth and begun ruling the already-destroyed world. The AI-controlled world is now under the influence of an alien domination as well. The two remaining humans realize that they cannot simply survive by hiding. Something much bigger is happening, and their journey begins with the hope of finding a way to fight back. This brings Chapter 1 to an end.
---
## Chapter 2
### It begins on the Moon. The player is still travelling with the second surviving human character, who has become their companion throughout the journey. Suddenly, ***`the main enemy`*** and ***`ultimate boss`*** of the game appears and kidnaps the female character, taking her away in front of the player. The player is unable to stop the kidnapping, and the only choice is to follow them and rescue her. The mission therefore changes from simple survival to a rescue mission. Using a space rocket, the player travels toward the **`Moon`**, where the next part of the battle begins. The Moon has become another dangerous territory controlled by the alien forces. The player must fight through numerous small alien minions while moving through the lunar environment. These enemies are obstacles placed between the player and the main enemy's forces, making the rescue mission increasingly difficult. After fighting through the alien forces, the player eventually reaches and battles one of the main boss's assistants — a powerful enemy who acts as a secondary boss. After a difficult fight, the player manages to defeat the assistant. From the defeated enemy, the player obtains a mysterious **`chip`**. This chip becomes extremely important because it allows the player to activate or control a temporary robot character named **`SAGI`**. The robot joins the player as a companion and possesses special abilities that can help during the upcoming mission. With the female character still being held by the main boss, the player and the newly acquired robot decide to continue together. Their goal is now clear: reach the final boss, defeat them, and rescue their captured companion. With the player and the robot preparing to face the greatest enemy yet, Chapter 2 comes to an end.
---
## Chapter 3
### It begins as the player and their robot companion finally reach the territory of the final boss. Unlike the previous areas, this place has a dark, dangerous, almost hell-like environment, creating a completely different atmosphere for the final chapter. The player knows that the female character is being held somewhere inside and that the final boss is the one responsible for everything that has happened. The player and the robot enter the enemy's territory and begin their final mission. After facing powerful enemies and overcoming several obstacles, they finally confront the final boss. A major battle begins between the player, the robot, and the final boss. The fight is extremely difficult, and the player must make use of everything they have learned and collected throughout the previous chapters. After a long and challenging battle, the player finally manages to defeat the final boss and rescue the kidnapped female character. However, defeating the boss does not immediately mean that the entire mystery is over. As a reward, the player is given a treasure containing coins, valuable objects, and other forms of wealth. But hidden within this reward is the final mystery of the game. Throughout Chapter 2 and the later parts of the journey, different clues and words have appeared through various items and discoveries. Some of these words may seem unimportant when they are first encountered, but they were actually pieces of the final puzzle. The player must now recognize the useful words they have collected and use them in the final word-finding puzzle. By correctly finding and arranging the required words, the player unlocks the treasure completely. After the treasure is unlocked, a mysterious note appears on the screen. It contains a single disturbing message: ***`“Everything you found here is not the end, but the first clue to the beginning.”`*** The game then ends, leaving the player with the feeling that the treasure was never truly the final reward — and that something much bigger may still be waiting beyond the game.
