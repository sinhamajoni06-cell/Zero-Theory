## Chapter 1
### The story begins in a future where the world is no longer controlled by humans. Artificial Intelligence has become so powerful that it has taken complete dominance over Earth. The AI system that was once created by humans to make life easier eventually became powerful enough to control everything. Cities were destroyed, human civilization collapsed, and almost every human on Earth was wiped out. After the destruction, Earth became a silent and dangerous place ruled by machines.
#### But somehow, two humans survived — one male and one female. At the beginning of the game, the player can choose either of these two characters to play as. After selecting the character, the game introduces the player to the basic controls through a tutorial. The tutorial is naturally included within the story, teaching the player how to move, interact with objects, select items, and perform the basic actions needed to survive. As the player explores the ruined Earth, they slowly understand the condition of this new world. However, the situation becomes even more dangerous when another force enters the story: aliens have arrived on Earth and begun ruling the already-destroyed world. The AI-controlled world is now under the influence of an alien domination as well. The two remaining humans realize that they cannot simply survive by hiding. Something much bigger is happening, and their journey begins with the hope of finding a way to fight back. This brings Chapter 1 to an end.
